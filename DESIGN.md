# Design System: Trust & Modernity

A modern, highly professional design system tailored for **The Ahmedabad Telephone Employees' Co-Operative Credit & Supply Society Limited**.

## Colors

- **Primary (Trust Blue)**: `#2563eb` (for primary actions, brand trust)
- **Secondary (Success Emerald)**: `#10b981` (for loan approvals, active accounts, and positive status)
- **Background (Dark Mode Canvas)**: `#0b0f19` (a deep slate-black)
- **Surface (Glass Panels)**: `#151c2c` (semi-transparent, combined with backdrop-filter blur)
- **Text Primary**: `#f8fafc`
- **Text Secondary**: `#94a3b8`

## Typography

- **Outfit** (Google Font): Used for high-level headings, brand elements, and main section titles to provide a premium fintech feel.
- **Inter** (Google Font): Used for body copy, data tables, member listings, and forms for maximum readability.

## Glassmorphism Rules

- **Backdrop Blur**: `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`
- **Border**: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
- **Shadow**: `0 10px 30px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`
